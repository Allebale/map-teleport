**Map Teleport** opens a map of Isle Delfino in any level and takes Mario straight to any level your save file has reached. It works in both Super Mario Sunshine and Super Mario Eclipse.

## What's new in 1.2.1
**Mod API:** 1.8.

- **Fixed:** the level pictures were missing when the game is in French, German, Spanish or Italian. They now show in every language.
- The game no longer says to rebuild the mod.

## What's new in 1.2.0
- **Button prompts for your controller:** a new setting, **Button prompts for**, names the buttons for an Xbox, PlayStation (X and O), Switch or GameCube controller, or the keyboard and mouse, on the map and on the episode select's Go back. Xbox by default.
- **Keyboard keys of your own:** set a key to teleport and a key to close in the mod's `settings.ini` (`key.teleport = Return`, `key.close = Backspace`, any key names). Until set, the prompts say **Not set**. **How to set the keyboard keys** in the Mods menu shows a guide with the file's place and the lines to add.

- **Delfino Airstrip and Corona Mountain:** the side panel now shows their Shine and coin record as well.

## What's new in 1.1.1
- **Straight back:** pressing **B** in a world's episode select now takes you straight back to the level you teleported from. The episode under the cursor no longer loads on the way, so there is no extra loading and no extra opening scene.

## What's new in 1.1.0
- **Shadow Mario first:** while Shadow Mario waits for you in Delfino Plaza, teleporting takes you to the plaza instead, so the story can't be skipped. In Super Mario Sunshine this covers the Yoshi's Egg chase (after Pinna Park episode 4), the Turbo Nozzle chase at 25 Shines and the Rocket Nozzle chase at 30 Shines. In Super Mario Eclipse it covers the nozzle chases at 25 and 30 Shines. The plaza shows in orange on the map. It can be turned off in the Mods menu.
- **B goes back:** pressing **B** in a world's episode select takes you back to the level you teleported from. It can be turned off in the Mods menu.
- **No openings when going back:** the opening scene of the level you return to is skipped.

### How it works
- Press **N** on the keyboard, or **D-pad up** on a controller, to open the map. The controller button can be changed or turned off in the Mods menu (F1).
- Move between levels with the stick, the D-pad or the arrow keys. **A** (left click) teleports, **B** (right click) closes the map.
- Choosing a world opens its own episode select, just like going through its painting or pipe. Press **B** there to go back to where you were. Delfino Plaza and Hotel Delfino follow the story as they do in the game.
- While Shadow Mario waits in Delfino Plaza, every teleport goes there first.
- The side panel shows a picture of the level, a short description, and your Shines and coin record there (in Super Mario Eclipse too, for its own levels as well).
- Mario can't be hurt while the map is open.

### Which levels you can go to
- **Super Mario Sunshine:** every level you have a Shine from, and every level you have been to since the mod was turned on.
- **Super Mario Eclipse:** every level you have been to since the mod was turned on, including its new worlds, pathways and the Daisy Cruiser worlds after the game is beaten.
- Levels you haven't reached are grey. The setting **Every level can be teleported to** opens all of them.
- Each save file's levels are remembered at once, even if you don't save the game.

Teleporting turns off achievements for that play, as the game's own warp menu does.

**Install:** unzip into your `mods` folder and turn it on in the Mods menu (F1).
